Player names

How to play

  1. Pass the phone around and let each player check their word in secret.
  2. Everyone gets the same Tamil word. The imposter doesn't know it.
  3. Take turns giving a short clue about the word. Don't say the word itself!
  4. When everyone has spoken, vote for who you think the imposter is.
  5. Catch the imposter and everyone else wins. If they escape, the imposter wins!
