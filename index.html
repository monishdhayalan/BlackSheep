How to play

  1. Pass the phone around. Each player privately looks at their word.
  2. Everyone gets the same Tamil word. The imposter doesn't know it, and may only get a hint.
  3. Take turns saying one clue about the word. Don't say the word itself!
  4. After discussing, vote for who you think the imposter is.
  5. Catch the imposter and everyone else wins. If they escape, the imposter wins!
